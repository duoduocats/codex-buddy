# 隐私 / Privacy

## 简体中文

- **登录与额度**：使用本机已有的 ChatGPT / Codex 登录状态查询额度和 Token 用量。登录信息不会发送给 GitHub，不读取聊天内容、项目代码或浏览器数据。
- **数据保存**：额度与用量结果只在应用运行时保留。应用保存你的设置和消息提醒状态。
- **网络连接**：额度与用量查询连接 ChatGPT；消息、检查更新和下载连接 GitHub。相关服务会收到 IP 地址、请求时间等必要网络信息。
- **用量图片**：图片在本机生成，包含所选日期范围的曲线和使用统计，不包含账号或登录信息。保存、复制或分享由你主动操作，分享对象和服务由你选择。
- **系统通知**：消息通知默认关闭。在设置开启消息提醒并允许 macOS 通知后生效。

反馈问题时，请先检查截图或日志，避免公开账号信息和登录令牌。安全问题请参阅 [安全反馈](../SECURITY.md)。

## English

- **Login and quota**: Your existing local ChatGPT / Codex login is used to check quota and token usage. Login information is not sent to GitHub. The app does not read conversations, project code, or browser data.
- **Stored data**: Quota and usage results are retained only while the app is running. The app saves your settings and message-reminder state.
- **Connections**: Quota and usage requests connect to ChatGPT. Messages, update checks, and downloads connect to GitHub. These services receive necessary connection information such as your IP address and request time.
- **Usage images**: Images are generated on your Mac and contain the selected chart range and usage statistics, without account or login information. You choose when to save, copy, or share, and which service or recipient to use.
- **System notifications**: Message notifications are off by default. Enable Message reminders in Settings and allow macOS notifications to receive them.

Before reporting a problem, check screenshots or logs for account information and login tokens. For security concerns, see [Security reporting](../SECURITY.md).
